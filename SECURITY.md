# Security policy

Find A Crib handles renters' accounts, saved searches and household income
details, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue.** Instead, either:

- use GitHub's **[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/divinedavis/Find-A-Crib/security/advisories/new)**, or
- email **divine@findacrib.com** with "Security" in the subject.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with
it. We'll acknowledge your report within 3 business days and keep you updated
until it's fixed.

## Scope

In scope: findacrib.com, the Find A Crib iOS app, and the
code in this repository.

Out of scope: denial-of-service, automated scanner output without a working
proof of concept, and social engineering.

Please don't access other people's data, degrade the service, or run
high-volume automated scans against production while testing.
