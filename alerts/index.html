What gets checked, and how often

  • NYC Housing Connect — the city's lottery, through HPD's own public API. Every 10 minutes.
  • HCR HousingSearch — state-supervised lotteries and Mitchell-Lama waitlists. Every hour.
  • Marketing agents' re-rental boards — the pages where HPD's pre-qualified firms post vacated affordable units. Every 2 hours during the day, from 9am to 9pm.
Why re-rentals matter. When a tenant moves out of an affordable unit, the apartment usually doesn't go back into a lottery. The agent re-rents it from their own website, and far fewer people know to look there. Every agent and their board →
