Visitors are counted from the site’s own nginx log — not from a JavaScript tag, so ad-blockers can’t hide them. Bots, Eric’s IPs and your own devices are removed: open nemoseamlessgutter.com/?owner=1 once on any phone or laptop you test from and it stops being counted there permanently, on every network it uses (?owner=0 undoes it). Otherwise a “visitor” is one IP + browser per day, since the site sets no tracking cookie: switching from wifi to cell counts twice, and a whole office behind one router counts once.
