NYC affordable-housing lottery agents

New York City doesn't run its housing lotteries itself. It hands each one to a marketing agent — a private firm or nonprofit on HPD's Pre-Qualified List that advertises the units, takes the NYC Housing Connect applications, verifies income, interviews applicants and signs the leases. If you're applying for a below-market apartment in a 421-a, 485-x, Inclusionary Housing or LIHTC building, one of these companies is the one holding your file.

Skip the lottery — look for re-rentals. When a tenant moves out of an affordable unit, the apartment usually doesn't go back into a lottery. The agent re-rents it directly, often first-come, first-served, on their own website. Far fewer people know to look there.
Heads up: an agent's company website is usually not that page — most are sales sites aimed at developers. Use the green link where there is one; otherwise the phone and email are your way in.
