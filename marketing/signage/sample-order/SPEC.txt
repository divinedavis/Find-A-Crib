THREE DESIGNS, THREE FILES — AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME FILE.
They are identical by eye on purpose. Only the QR pattern differs, because
each one encodes a different address. Verified before sending:

  findacrib-card-a1-100x160mm-nfc.pdf  ->  findacrib.com/c/a1
  findacrib-card-a2-100x160mm-nfc.pdf  ->  findacrib.com/c/a2
  findacrib-card-a7-100x160mm-nfc.pdf  ->  findacrib.com/c/a7

Scan each PDF with a phone to confirm — the last two characters of the
address differ. They must stay separated and labelled at every step.

  findacrib-card-a1-100x160mm-nfc.pdf    "a1"
  findacrib-card-a2-100x160mm-nfc.pdf    "a2"
  findacrib-card-a7-100x160mm-nfc.pdf    "a7"

THE ONE THING THAT MUST NOT CHANGE
  The QR is dark on light and sits on the white half of the card. A code
  printed light-on-dark is an inverted code: recent iPhones read them,
  older Android cameras do not. Do not "improve" the contrast by moving
  the code onto the blue.

AFTER DELIVERY
  Write the NFC chips ourselves, one URL per design:
    a1 -> https://findacrib.com/t/a1
    a2 -> https://findacrib.com/t/a2
    a7 -> https://findacrib.com/t/a7
  The printed codes already carry /c/a1, /c/a2, /c/a7 — a tap and a scan
  are counted separately on purpose.
