Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026. Applies to findacrib.com and the Find A Crib app for iPhone. Replaces the versions dated July 16 and September 2, 2026.

Find A Crib shows public records about rent-regulated apartment buildings — the state's rent-stabilization register, HPD housing-code data, HUD rent estimates, advertised rents and affordable-housing lotteries. This page lists everything we collect about the people who use it, why, and who else sees it.

Who else sees data

  • Google — the website runs Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion measurement and Google AdSense ads. These set Google cookies and send Google the pages you visit, and AdSense may use them to choose which ads to show. You can turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com. Google Sign-In and Google Maps are also Google services. The iPhone app contains no Google analytics or ads.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, push notifications, and the app's maps and street-level photos.
  • Supabase (on Amazon Web Services, United States) — hosts our accounts and database.
  • Stripe — website payments.
  • NYC Open Data — when you open a building's violations or complaints, your browser asks the City's public data portal for that building's records directly. No personal information is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not give your email, income, rent limit or voucher status to landlords, agents, data brokers or advertisers.
  • The iPhone app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • Only public records appear on building pages. Nothing about you appears on them except comments you choose to post.

Published statistics

We publish reports such as the rent report, built from public listings and from our usage data added up across everyone — for example, "the most-viewed neighborhoods this month." We only publish a count if it covers at least 10 different visitors, and a published figure never includes an email address, an account, a single person's search, or any income or rent a person entered for alerts.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, Profile → Delete account; on the website, sign in and use Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, saves, saved searches, home building, alert settings, subscription record, push tokens, comments and likes. Usage events are kept for statistics but disconnected from you: the link to your account is erased.
  • Lottery alerts: every email has a one-click unsubscribe. To have your alert signup — including any income or rent you entered — deleted entirely, email us.
  • Usage events in the app: Profile → turn off Share anonymous usage, and the app stops sending them.
  • On the website: clearing this site's data resets your visitor ID. Browser tracking protection and ad blockers stop the Google tags.
  • A copy or correction of your data: email us and we will reply within 30 days.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Lottery alert signups are kept until you ask us to delete them. Usage events are kept for statistics and, after an account is deleted, are no longer linked to it.

Children

Find A Crib is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Material changes will be noted in the app and on the site.
