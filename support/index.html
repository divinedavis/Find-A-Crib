Support

Find A Crib is an informational tool, not a broker or landlord. We don't list apartments or take applications.

What am I looking at?

Every building on New York State's rent-stabilization register (about 47,000), with HPD violation and complaint counts, the neighborhood's typical rent from HUD, rents advertised in the last 5 days, buildings that take Section 8 vouchers, and open lotteries and waitlists from HousingSearch.ny.gov. Read what rent stabilization means and how to check your own apartment.

Signing in

Sign in with Apple or Google — no passwords. Your saved buildings sync between the app and findacrib.com. If Sign in with Apple shows "Sign Up Not Completed", that is an Apple-side error: re-enter your Apple Account password in Settings, or try again later.

Find A Crib Plus

Plus unlocks managing-agent phone numbers, saved searches, listing alerts and landlord research. Manage or cancel a web subscription from Profile on findacrib.com; an App Store subscription is managed in your iPhone's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Deleting your account

App: Profile → Delete account. Web: Profile → Delete account. It is immediate and permanent. See the privacy policy.

Something wrong with a building's data?

The register comes from NYS Homes and Community Renewal and violations from NYC HPD; we show what they publish. If a building looks wrong, email us the address and we'll look.
