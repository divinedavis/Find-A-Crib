What Find A Crib is

Find A Crib is an informational tool that maps NYC rent-stabilized buildings using public government data. We are not a broker, landlord, or real-estate agent, and we do not list, rent, or manage apartments. We do not guarantee that any building has an available unit.

Accuracy of information

Building information — including addresses, unit counts, rent estimates, HPD violations, registered owners, and managing agents — is drawn from public sources (NYC PLUTO, NYC Open Data / HPD, HUD, DHCR) and third-party listing data. It may be incomplete, out of date, or contain errors. Rent estimates are ZIP-code typical ranges from HUD Fair Market Rents and are not a specific building's actual rent. Verify all information independently before making housing decisions. The Service is provided for general information only and is not legal, financial, or housing advice.
