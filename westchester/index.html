Find A Crib

Find rent-stabilized apartments in Yonkers and Westchester

Outside New York City, rent stabilization is the Emergency Tenant Protection Act (ETPA), which generally covers buildings of six or more units built before 1974 in the localities that adopted it. Find A Crib maps every building registered with NYS Homes and Community Renewal under ETPA in Westchester County, with the year each registration was last filed. Also on Find A Crib: rent-stabilized NYC, rent-controlled San Francisco, rent-stabilized (RSO) Los Angeles and rent-controlled Washington DC.
